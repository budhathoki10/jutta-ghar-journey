# GoGo Jutta Ghar — Brand Foundation

## Positioning
For busy Kathmandu residents who need shoe care and a flawless fit, GoGo Jutta Ghar is the local shoe house on Jagatsundar Marg that blends hand-finished comfort with city-ready confidence.

## Archetype
- Primary: Artisan — every service is grounded in craft, measured fit and the slow joy of a well-made pair.
- Supporting: Lover — the brand feels warm, tactile and quietly caring about daily life in Kathmandu.

## Voice & tone
- Confident: We speak with quiet authority about what shoes should feel like.
- Grounded: We describe real streets, real wear and real people.
- Tactile: We name textures, touch and fit with simple precision.
- Local: We make Kathmandu feel visible without sounding like a tourist brochure.

| We say | We don’t say |
|---|---|
| "Bring your city walk to the shop and we’ll match it." | "Transform your footwear lifestyle." |
| "This fit holds through the monsoon." | "This is the future of footwear." |
| "We measure both feet and the way you move." | "We optimize your shoe journey." |
| "Shop service on Jagatsundar Marg." | "A one-stop global solution." |
| "Ask for a rainy-day repair slot." | "No compromise, no exceptions." |

## Name treatment
Use the wordmark in mixed case: **GoGo Jutta Ghar**.
- Use uppercase for navigation and compact labels: `GO GO JUTTA GHAR`.
- Use mixed case for editorial headlines, the primary logo lockup, and brand storytelling.
- Treat `GoGo` as the rhythmic lead, with `Jutta Ghar` as the supporting signature.

## Design tokens
- Primary: warm terracotta for action and focus.
- Deep neutral: soft off-black for typography and anchored surfaces.
- Soft neutral: creamy paper for backgrounds and generous white space.
- Accent: golden ochre for subtle highlights.
- Signal: muted forest for confirmations and directional links.

Keep the system quiet, editorial and layered rather than bright or flashy. The experience should feel like a flagship shoe house, not a generic store page.
